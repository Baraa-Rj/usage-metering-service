# DocuFlow Onboarding Guide

Welcome to DocuFlow. This guide covers your first week.

## Day one
- Collect your laptop and badge from the front desk before 10:00.
- Sign in to the company account and enable two-factor authentication.
- Join the #general and #help channels; ask anything in #help.

## Your first week
Your manager schedules a 30-minute check-in on Monday, Wednesday and Friday.
By Friday you should have shipped one small change to production with a buddy:
a typo fix or a log message is enough. The goal is to learn the release path,
not to build a feature.

## Tools
We track work in the planning board, review code in pull requests, and write
decisions down in short design notes. Every pull request needs one approval and
green tests. Deploys happen automatically from the main branch.

## Time off and expenses
Request time off at least two weeks ahead in the HR portal. Expenses under 50 EUR
need no approval; keep the receipt and submit it within 30 days.
