# Q3 Sales Report

## Headline numbers
- Revenue: 1.84M EUR, up 12% on Q2 and 31% on Q3 last year.
- New customers: 146 (Q2: 118). Churned customers: 19 (Q2: 24).
- Net revenue retention: 114%.
- Average contract value: 12,600 EUR, up from 11,900 EUR.

## By segment
Mid-market remained the largest segment with 58% of revenue. Enterprise grew fastest
(+27% quarter on quarter) after two six-figure contracts with logistics companies.
Small business revenue was flat; self-serve sign-ups rose but converted at a lower rate
after the free tier was extended from 14 to 30 days.

## By region
Germany and the Netherlands delivered 61% of new bookings. The Nordics pipeline doubled,
but most of those deals are expected to close in Q1 next year. Southern Europe missed
its target by 18% because two partner resellers paused activity during restructuring.

## What worked
The document-processing usage dashboard, launched in July, shortened sales cycles: deals
that included a usage review closed in 34 days on average versus 51 days without one.
Annual prepaid plans with a pooled monthly allowance were chosen by 40% of new customers.

## Risks for Q4
- Three enterprise renewals (together 210k EUR) are due in December.
- Pricing pressure from a competitor offering unlimited processing on its top tier.
- Hiring: two of four planned account executives have started.

## Outlook
We forecast 1.95M to 2.05M EUR for Q4, assuming the enterprise renewals close on time.
