# Information Security Policy (summary)

## Accounts and passwords
Every employee uses a personal account; shared logins are not allowed.
Passwords are stored only in the company password manager and must be at least
14 characters. Two-factor authentication is mandatory for email, source control,
the cloud console and the HR portal.

## Devices
Laptops use full-disk encryption and lock after 5 minutes of inactivity.
Operating system updates must be installed within 7 days of release.
Lost or stolen devices are reported to security@docuflow.example within one hour,
so the device can be wiped remotely.

## Customer data
Customer documents are processed only inside the production environment.
Copying customer data to laptops, personal cloud storage or chat tools is forbidden.
Access to production requires a ticket and expires after 8 hours.

## Incidents
If you suspect an incident (a phishing email you clicked, a leaked key, unusual
activity), report it immediately in #security-incidents. Do not try to investigate
alone and do not delete evidence. Reporting quickly is never punished.

## Reviews
Access rights are reviewed every quarter. This policy is reviewed once a year by
the security team and approved by the CTO.
